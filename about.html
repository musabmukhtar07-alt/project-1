About this project

A fan’s tribute
to the game.

The Number 10 is a small, independent fan-made website celebrating Lionel Messi’s extraordinary football journey—from Rosario to the world stage.

These pages bring together a career timeline, memorable goals and a selection of team honours. They’re made for fans who love the craft: the close control, the vision, the unhurried finish—and the joy football can bring to millions.

Made with care

This is an unofficial tribute and is not affiliated with Lionel Messi, his representatives, any club, league or governing body. Names and competition references are used for editorial identification. No official photos or trademarks are used in this handcrafted design.
