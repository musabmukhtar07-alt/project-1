to the top.

Greatness was never one moment. It was thousands of them: the early starts, the brave choices, and the joy of having a ball at his feet.

1987 — 2000

A dream begins in Rosario

Lionel Andrés Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. He grew up playing football with his brothers and cousins. At Newell’s Old Boys, his talent was already impossible to miss.

ARGENTINA · THE BEGINNING
2000 — 2004

A leap across the ocean

At 13, Messi moved with his family to Barcelona and joined the club’s famed youth academy, La Masia. He made his first-team debut at 16, beginning a bond with the club that would define an era.

LA MASIA · FIRST TEAM DEBUT
2005 — 2012

Barcelona finds its maestro

With quick feet and a remarkable eye for the game, Messi became the heartbeat of a great Barcelona side. In 2009, the club won six major trophies in one calendar year. He claimed four consecutive Ballon d’Or awards from 2009 to 2012.

THE NUMBER 10 · A NEW ERA
2014 — 2021

Carrying the sky blue and white

Argentina reached the 2014 World Cup final. The international wait continued, then ended in 2021 when Messi lifted the Copa América in Brazil, his first major senior title with the national team.

COPA AMÉRICA · 2021
2022

The dream comes home

In Qatar, Messi led Argentina to the World Cup title, scoring in the final and winning the Golden Ball as the tournament’s best player. In Buenos Aires, millions poured into the streets to welcome their champions.

FIFA WORLD CUP · CHAMPION
2023 — TODAY

A new chapter in Miami

Messi began a new chapter with Inter Miami in 2023, helping the club win its first Leagues Cup. Wherever he plays, the familiar magic travels with him—and inspires a new generation to pick up a ball.

INTER MIAMI · THE STORY CONTINUES
Qatar · 18 December 2022

One last
piece of the puzzle.

After a final for the ages against France, Argentina won the World Cup on penalties. Messi lifted the trophy that had eluded him, completing a journey that began on the pitches of Rosario.
